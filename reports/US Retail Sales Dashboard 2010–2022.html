Key summary observations

Findings from the notebook, 2010–2022.

Automotive dominates and accelerated after 2020Dipped to ~$4.46M in 2020 (over 36% of top-5 revenue) then surged past $5.6M by 2022. Peak quarter was Q3 in 2020 ($1.23M) and Q2 in 2021–22. Its four sub-categories are the top 4 business kinds ($51.2M, 26.5% of all sales).
Food & Beverage: steady, recession-proof growthLinear climb from ~$2.0M (2010) to over $3.2M (2022) with no 2020 decline. Quarterly sales stayed within $708K–$753K in 2020, a 6.23% spread, the most dependable baseline revenue.
Restaurants & Bars: most pandemic-exposed, strong recovery~$2.2M in 2019, down to ~$1.8M in 2020 under lockdowns, then back to ~$2.5M+ by 2022.
Home Goods & Electronics: seasonal Q4 momentumGrew 50.82% from Q1 ($338,240) to Q4 ($510,127) in 2020. It and General Merchandise peaked in Q4 in every year from 2018 to 2022.
Essential retail is the secondary revenue clusterFood and beverage stores ($9.45M, 4.9%), grocery stores ($8.46M, 4.4%) and supermarkets ($8.09M, 4.2%) show resilient demand; motor vehicle and parts dealers is the single largest kind at $14.5M (7.5%).

Strategies in place

Recommended actions from the notebook, tied to the observations above.

1 · Prioritize Automotive and Food & BeverageFocus operational support and capital allocation on the two industries that generate the bulk of revenue; both represent essential, high-volume consumer spending.
2 · Invest in the Automotive supply chainGiven the rapid post-2020 acceleration, direct capital and marketing toward dealer inventory and parts supply-chain optimization.
3 · Use Food & Beverage as the defensive hedgeIts steady, linear growth was unaffected by global disruption, making it the core anchor in economic downturns.
4 · Build flexibility into Restaurants & BarsAdopt flexible operating structures, such as hybrid delivery models, to cushion sudden demand shocks like COVID-19.
5 · Recommended asset allocationCore anchor, 40%: Food & Beverage (capital preservation, minimal quarterly variance). Growth engine, 35%: Automotive (high throughput, strong Q3–Q4 cycles). Opportunistic, 15%: Home Goods & Electronics (Q3–Q4 surge; plan Q4 holiday inventory).
